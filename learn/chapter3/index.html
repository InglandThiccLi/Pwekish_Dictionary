Chapter 3: Tenses, Adjectives, Nouns and Suffixes

Introduction

We had just gone through a tough journey in learning the PVIM of Pwekish. It's time to take a rest and learn something simpler. We will talk about tenses first. Tenses in Pwekish is considered to be easier than English. It's just about adding prefixes and irregularities generally do not exist. Next, we will have adjectives, adverbs and gerunds. They are very useful and basic parts of speech in Pwekish. It's difficult to avoid using them when we are going to talk about something more specific and complex. Finally, we will learn some common suffixes and drill deeper into the world of agglutinative language. We guarantee it is a simple but at the same time a fun process to create or decipher new words with suffixes you learn.
