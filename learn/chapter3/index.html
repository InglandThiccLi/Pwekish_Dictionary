Chapter 3: Tenses, Adjectives, Nouns and Suffixes

Introduction

We have just finished a tough journey learning the PVIM of Pwekish. It is time to take a break and learn something a little simpler!

First, we will talk about tenses. Tenses in Pwekish are considered much easier than in English; it is simply a matter of adding prefixes, and irregularities generally do not exist.

Next, we will introduce adjectives, adverbs, and gerunds. These are highly useful, fundamental parts of speech in Pwekish, and it is difficult to avoid using them when trying to communicate more specific and complex ideas.

Finally, we will learn some common suffixes and dive deeper into the world of agglutinative languages. We guarantee it will be a simple yet fun process to create and decipher brand-new words using the suffixes you learn!
